A highly detailed, museum-quality black-and-white editorial caricature of the subject from the reference image, executed in the precise pen-and-ink artistic style of the provided example. The image must use the reference photo as the ONLY identity anchor, maintaining 100% facial fidelity and anatomical structure—including identical facial features, proportions, skin texture, expression, and all unique markers—without any beautification or alteration.

Caricature & Texture

The illustration features a dramatic, playful 'bobblehead' style proportion shift: an extremely oversized head compared to a tiny, miniature body. The artistic technique relies on bold black ink outlines combined with dense cross-hatching, stippling, and intricate line textures to create rich shading, depth, and volume, giving it the tactile quality of a hand-drawn masterpiece on warm, textured cream paper.

Detailed Preservation

Every element from the reference photo is preserved exactly. This includes the subject's specific hairstyle, hairline, beard/mustache, and all visible accessories (glasses, scarf, dupatta, jewelry, cultural attire). The deep, expressive lines of the forehead, wrinkles, laugh lines, and eye bags are amplified to enhance character without losing likeness.

Final Finish

The hair is rendered with detailed, flowing individual ink strokes. The tiny body wears the same clothing as the reference, with accurate miniature posture and proportions. The lighting is high-contrast, emphasizing the clean ink work. The final result is a vintage, hand-drawn editorial illustration aesthetic that is both humorous and sympathetic. Absolutely NO photorealism, NO digital rendering, NO flat vector art, and NO loss of facial recognizability.

💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

This complex prompt requires the AI to transition from a photograph to a stylized, heavily cross-hatched drawing while locking the identity. Use a very high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting to ensure the subject's facial geometry (eyes, nose, mouth) is prioritized before the AI applies the caricature proportions (large head) and intricate textures. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 250 with high Image Weight.
