A sophisticated modern geometric editorial portrait (vertical 4:5 aspect ratio) presented as a close-up bust portrait from a slightly low angle, where the face dominates the frame. The artwork must strictly preserve the subject's identity, key facial features, expression, and overall likeness, translating them into a semi-abstract style using simplified geometric forms and sculpted planes.

Stylization & Geometry

The face and upper torso are constructed from elegant polygonal and curved planes, utilizing large simplified volumes instead of microscopic skin details. Depth is created through overlapping geometric shadow masses and large, smooth shadow planes. The expression features a warm, charismatic, and large expressive smile with slightly exaggerated eyes for personality.

Rendering & Surfaces

Executed as a digital vector painting with painterly gradient rendering. Features clean faceted geometry with crisp yet organic edges and silky airbrushed transitions. The surfaces possess an extremely subtle paper grain texture with invisible brushwork, resulting in a premium editorial poster finish.

Lighting & Color Palette

Illuminated by soft cinematic studio lighting with diffused, medium-high contrast, large smooth geometric shadow planes, and soft satin highlights with gentle gradient transitions. The restrained, high-contrast cool-with-warm-highlights color palette includes deep navy blue, warm ivory, soft cream, charcoal blue, muted slate, and a single bold accent red.

Clothing & Background

Clothing is rendered in large clean color blocks with simplified geometric folds and minimal seams. The background is minimal, featuring soft abstract overlapping circles, clean negative space, and a subtle atmospheric gradient. Absolutely NO photorealism, NO real skin texture, NO messy brushwork, and NO flat vector art.

💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

This stylized portrait requires a balance between geometric abstraction and identity preservation. Use a high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting to lock the subject's facial structure before the AI applies the polygonal planes and airbrushed gradients. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 200 with high Image Weight (--iw 2.0+) to maintain likeness while achieving the geometric style.
