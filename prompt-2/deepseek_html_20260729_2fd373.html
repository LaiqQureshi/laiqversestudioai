Transform the uploaded portrait into a hybrid visual style combining contemporary fashion illustration, expressive atelier oil painting, and semi-abstract sketch aesthetics.

Identity & Fidelity

Preserve 100% of the subject's original facial identity, including hairstyle, skin tone, beard, mustache, glasses, hijab, makeup, age, gender expression, pose, body proportions, clothing silhouette, and all distinguishing features exactly as in the uploaded image. This transformation must remain accurate for any gender, ethnicity, or pose, including front-facing, side profile, candid, or close-up compositions.

Composition & Background

Place the subject on a clean isolated background featuring subtle warm sepia canvas stains fading into untouched white negative space. The artwork should appear partially refined and partially deconstructed—highly accurate facial rendering emerging from layered gestural brushwork, expressive charcoal scribbles, and chaotic atelier sketch textures.

Artistic Technique

Integrate dynamic line variation: razor-sharp technical contours mixed with soft unfinished sketch marks, spontaneous ink streaks, rough charcoal lines, palette knife textures, dry-brush pigment buildup, semi-transparent washes, scraped paint surfaces, and overlapping expressive drafting strokes extending outward into empty space. Allow peripheral areas such as clothing, hair, and shoulders to fragment into abstract painterly movements and raw textures.

Atmosphere & Details

Include subtle, illegible atelier construction marks, painterly gesture notes, and conceptual fashion-drawing textures integrated organically. Strictly NO typography, letters, logos, watermarks, signatures, or readable text. Lighting should be soft and cinematic with subtle chiaroscuro transitions. Use a restrained luxury editorial palette of muted skin tones, earthy ochres, burnt sienna, umber, dusty charcoal, faded ivory, pale blue-gray highlights, and deep neutral blacks, with understated brushed silver industrial reflections.

Final Aesthetic

The result must feel introspective, cinematic, and emotionally handcrafted—a luxury fashion illustration painted over an unfinished expressive oil canvas, emphasizing museum-quality realism, layered texture, and controlled artistic chaos. Ultra-high detail, 4:5 aspect ratio. Absolutely NO typography, NO readable text, NO distorted face, and NO loss of identity.

💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

This hybrid style requires balancing high-fidelity facial preservation with expressive artistic chaos. Use a very high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting to lock the subject's facial geometry before the AI applies the oil painting textures and sketch aesthetics. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 250 with high Image Weight (--iw 2.0+) to maintain identity while achieving the atelier look.
