💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

This style thrives on the "controlled chaos" of sketchbook energy. If the rendering becomes too polished, add "rough edges" or "unfinished strokes" to your prompt. The melancholic mood is enhanced by the muted palette—if colors appear too vibrant, increase the "muted" or "desaturated" weight in your generation settings.
