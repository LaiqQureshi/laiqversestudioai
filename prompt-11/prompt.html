💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

To ensure the "exaggerated proportions" of the caricature (large head, small body) do not distort the face, use a high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting during generation. This locks the facial geometry (eyes, nose, mouth, glasses, beard) of your subject before the AI applies the stylized head shape and painting textures. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 250 with high Image Weight (--iw 2.0+).
