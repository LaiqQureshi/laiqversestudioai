💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

When generating illustrations that combine complex poses (like playing an instrument) with strict facial preservation, ensure you use a high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting so the face from the reference image remains untouched while the AI interprets the painterly sepia style and guitar elements. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 250 with high Image Weight (--iw 2.0+).
