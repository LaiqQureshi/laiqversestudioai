Subject Details

The subject wears the same transparent-frame glasses as seen in the input image and possesses a gentle, confident smile. He is dressed in a simple black T-shirt layered beneath a black overcoat.

Pose & Perspective

The pose is a serious, dramatic side-look profile. The subject is positioned to the right of the frame, looking intently toward the left.

Lighting & Atmosphere

The scene is set against a deep, moody crimson studio background. The lighting is highly cinematic and complex: a sharp, dramatic red rim light defines the profile of his hair and coat, separating him from the background. A soft, warm key light subtly illuminates the far side of his face, maintaining detail and a gentle smile while creating deep, high-contrast shadows (chiaroscuro effect) across the near side.

Technical Finish

Captured with an 85mm lens, resulting in an ultra-shallow depth of field. The focus is razor-sharp on the subject's eye and skin texture (pores, subtle imperfections, beard detail). The final render is 8k, embodying the quality of a high-end, photorealistic fashion editorial. Absolutely NO photorealistic skin smoothing, NO airbrushing, NO plastic texture, and NO loss of facial recognizability.

💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

This is a complex portrait that merges high identity preservation with dramatic, directional lighting and specific eyewear. To ensure the final result is perfect, use a very high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting to lock the subject's facial geometry before the AI applies the red rim lighting and side-profile pose. If the glasses disappear or warp, increase the strength or use an image-based identity reference tool. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 200 with high Image Weight (--iw 2.0+).
