💡 Pro-Tip for Best Results

This complex prompt requires the AI to transition from a photograph to a stylized 3D character while locking the identity. Use a very high "Character Strength" (or "Identity Weight") setting to ensure the facial geometry of your subject is locked before the AI applies the caricature proportions (large head/hands) and cinematic textures. For Midjourney, use --style raw --s 250 with high Image Weight.
